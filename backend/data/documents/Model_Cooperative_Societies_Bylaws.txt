PRIMARY AGRICULTURAL CREDIT COOPERATIVE SOCIETIES (PACS)
MODEL BY-LAWS & GOVERNANCE FRAMEWORK
MINISTRY OF COOPERATION

Chapter I: Preliminary and Definitions
Rule 2: Definitions and Operational Area
1. "Society" means the Primary Agricultural Credit Society registered under the State Cooperative Societies Act.
2. "Member" means an individual farmer or rural artisan duly admitted into membership in accordance with these By-laws.
3. "Managing Committee" means the governing body of the Society entrusted with the direction and management of affairs.
4. The area of operation of the Society shall be defined by village boundaries specified in Schedule A of the registration certificate.

Chapter III: Membership Eligibility and Shares
Rule 14: Eligibility Criteria for Membership
An individual shall be eligible for admission as an ordinary member of the Society if he/she satisfies all of the following conditions:
a) Is a citizen of India and has attained 18 years of age.
b) Is of sound mind and not an undischarged insolvent.
c) Has agricultural land holdings, is an operational tenant farmer, or pursues allied agricultural activities within the operational area of the Society.
d) Has purchased at least one full share valued at Rs. 100/- and paid the non-refundable entrance fee of Rs. 10/-.
e) Has not been convicted of any offense involving moral turpitude within the preceding 5 years.
No member shall be permitted to hold more than one-fifth (20%) of the total issued share capital of the Society.

Chapter IV: Voting Rights and General Body
Rule 19: Democratic Control and Voting
1. Every ordinary member shall have exactly one vote, irrespective of the number of shares held, embodying the principle of "One Member, One Vote".
2. No member shall be allowed to vote by proxy at any meeting of the General Body.
3. Disqualification of Voting Rights: A member shall forfeit voting rights and the right to contest elections if:
   i) The member has defaulted on repayment of any loan or interest dues to the Society for a continuous period exceeding 12 months.
   ii) The member has failed to patronize the minimum business services of the Society (minimum Rs. 1,000 credit or fertilizer transaction) during the preceding financial year.
   iii) The member has failed to attend at least three consecutive Annual General Meetings (AGMs) without sanctioned leave.

Chapter VI: Management Committee and Board Composition
Rule 27: Board Representation and Quorum
1. The Managing Committee shall consist of 11 elected members, of which:
   a) At least two seats shall be reserved for Women members.
   b) At least one seat shall be reserved for Scheduled Castes (SC) or Scheduled Tribes (ST) members.
   c) One seat shall be reserved for Small and Marginal farmers having landholding below 2 hectares.
2. The term of the Managing Committee shall be five years from the date of election.
3. The quorum for any meeting of the Managing Committee shall be one-half of the total members, or five members, whichever is higher.
4. The quorum for the Annual General Body Meeting (AGM) shall be one-third of the total voting members or 100 members, whichever is less.

Chapter VIII: Net Profit Allocation and Dividend Rules
Rule 38: Disposal of Net Profits
The net profits of the Society as audited by the Cooperative Department shall be allocated strictly in the following sequence:
1. Not less than 25% shall be credited to the Statutory Reserve Fund.
2. Not less than 10% shall be allocated to the Bad and Doubtful Debt Fund.
3. A contribution of 1% to the Cooperative Education Fund maintained by the State Cooperative Union.
4. The maximum dividend payable to ordinary members on their paid-up share capital shall not exceed 15% per annum, subject to the prior written approval of the Registrar of Cooperative Societies.
