RESERVE BANK OF INDIA & NABARD GUIDELINES
KISAN CREDIT CARD (KCC) AND INTEREST SUBVENTION NORMS

Chapter II: Target Beneficiaries and Eligibility
Norm 3: Eligible Categories of Borrowers
1. All farmers — individual or joint borrowers who are owner-cultivators.
2. Tenant farmers, oral lessees, and sharecroppers cultivating leased agricultural land.
3. Self Help Groups (SHGs) or Joint Liability Groups (JLGs) of farmers including tenant farmers.
4. Beneficiaries undertaking allied agricultural activities: Dairy, Fishery, Poultry, and Animal Husbandry.
No collateral security or mortgage of agricultural land is required for crop loans sanctioned up to Rs. 1,60,000/-.

Chapter III: Credit Limit Determination and Scale of Finance
Norm 6: Assessment of Scale of Finance
The credit limit under Kisan Credit Card for the first year is fixed based on:
a) Cultivated acreage multiplied by the District Level Technical Committee (DLTC) Scale of Finance for each crop.
b) Plus 10% of limit towards post-harvest, household, and consumption requirements.
c) Plus 20% of limit towards repair and maintenance expenses of farm assets plus crop insurance premium.
The KCC card is valid for 5 years, with a built-in automatic 10% annual escalation in loan limit to counter inflation.

Chapter V: Modified Interest Subvention Scheme (MISS)
Norm 12: Concessional Interest Rates and Prompt Repayment Incentive
1. The standard lending rate charged by Commercial Banks, RRBs, and Cooperative Banks for short-term crop loans is 9.0% per annum.
2. The Government of India provides an upfront Interest Subvention of 1.5% to 2.0% per annum to financial institutions.
3. Prompt Repayment Incentive (PRI): An additional subvention of 3.0% per annum is provided to farmers who repay their short-term crop loans within the due date (maximum 1 year from disbursement).
4. As a result, farmers repaying on or before the due date effectively borrow at an ultra-low net interest rate of 4.0% per annum for loan limits up to Rs. 3,00,000/-.
5. In case of natural calamities where loan rescheduling is sanctioned, the interest subvention benefit is extended for the first year of the rescheduled term.
